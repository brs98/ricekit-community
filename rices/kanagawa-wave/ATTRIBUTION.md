# Kanagawa screenshot

`screenshots/desktop.png` is the upstream Kanagawa screenshot by Tommaso
Laurenzi, distributed under the MIT License. Source:
https://github.com/rebelot/kanagawa.nvim/blob/master/screenshot.png

The screenshot is a representative palette preview. Clean-account screenshots
of the complete Rice remain part of the macOS release acceptance gate.

The applied wallpaper is packaged with the `kanagawa` theme. Its Public Domain
Open Access source and exact digest are recorded in
`themes/kanagawa/ATTRIBUTION.md`.
